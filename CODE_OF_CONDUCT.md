# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participation in this project must be respectful, constructive, and welcoming. Examples of expected
behavior include:

- Giving actionable technical feedback and accepting it gracefully.
- Respecting different experience levels, backgrounds, and viewpoints.
- Keeping discussions focused on the project and its users.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and helping repair their impact.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, personal
attacks, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable in any project space.

## Scope

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, code review, release coordination, and
other spaces where someone represents this project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository’s
[private reporting form](https://github.com/mzubair481/express-boilerplate/security/advisories/new)
with “Code of Conduct” at the start of the title. Do not include sensitive personal information
beyond what is necessary to investigate the report.

The maintainer may edit or remove contributions, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently
ban participants when behavior is harmful. Reports will be reviewed as confidentially and
impartially as practical. Retaliation against a reporter is prohibited.
